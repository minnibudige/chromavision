ABOUT OUR STARTUP

Reimagining How Humankind Experiences Light

Born from engineering curiosity to solve one of the most frustrating everyday compromises in optical history.

THE ORIGIN STORY

Why ChromaVision Was Imagined

Over 4.5 billion people worldwide require vision correction to navigate their daily lives. Yet for decades, prescription wearers have faced a persistent, frustrating dilemma whenever stepping into daylight.

Traditional options force wearers into awkward workarounds: carrying two separate cases everywhere, fumbling with clunky clip-ons, or wearing first-generation photochromic lenses that remain darkened inside air-conditioned rooms, take up to 10 minutes to clear, and completely ignore ambient temperature.

"Why should someone who requires prescription clarity have to sacrifice effortless sun protection, aesthetic style, or thermal comfort? We asked: what if a single lens could respond organically to its environment?"

— The ChromaVision Student Founder Collective

Our Startup Vision

To eliminate optical compromise forever. We envision a future where high-fashion aesthetic design, medical-grade ophthalmic prescription accuracy, and responsive nanomaterials merge effortlessly into a timeless everyday accessory.

Our Startup Mission

To engineer and validate smarter, zero-battery adaptive eyewear. Through student-led research in molecular polymer physics, we seek to replace bulky electronic smart glasses with elegant, naturally responsive materials accessible to everyone.

PROPOSED TECHNOLOGY

The Dual-Matrix Molecular Paradigm

A fusion of thermo-responsive crystalline chemistry and photo-kinetic solar attenuation — engineered into a unified optical substrate.

01

Prescription Optical Integration

Custom-cast directly on high-index (1.67 / 1.74) optical polymer. Accommodates single-vision, bifocal, and progressive prescriptions without chromatic aberration or edge distortion.

Rx Range: -8.00D to +6.00D Abbe Value: 42
02

Temperature-Responsive Chromic Matrix

Organic micro-encapsulated thermochromic pigments shift light reflection wavelengths dynamically: frosty Ice Blue in chill, refined Silver Grey in mild climates, and rich Bronze in warm conditions.

Cold: <12°C (Ice Blue) Warm: >28°C (Bronze)
03

Sunlight Solar Override

When exposed to intense UV sunlight, photo-reactive silver halide nano-compounds cross-link immediately. This overrides the temperature tint, transforming the lens into Deep Blackout Mode for glare protection.

Override Trigger: UV ≥ 6 Absorption: 92%
04

Zero-Button Autonomous Physics

Operates entirely via passive molecular kinetics. No fragile lithium-ion batteries, zero magnetic charging pins, no paired apps, and no electromagnetic fields near the head.

Weight: 19 grams 0 mAh Required
05

UV400 Total Bio-Shield

Continuous medical-grade ocular preservation blocking 100% of UVA and UVB rays up to 400nm across all tint states — guarding corneal tissue and the macula from photo-oxidative stress.

100% UVA / UVB Block ANSI Z80.3 Ready

Proposed 6-Layer Nano-Composite Structure

Theoretical molecular stack engineered for durability, optical clarity, and continuous environmental adaptation.

L1
Hydrophobic & Oleophobic Nano-Armor

Repels rain droplets, fingerprint smudges, and dust particles while resisting scratches.

L2
Photonic Solar-Darkening Matrix

Ultraviolet-responsive silver halide nanoparticles responsible for the intense solar blackout override.

L3
Thermo-Responsive Chromic Core

Micro-encapsulated thermotropic liquid crystal layer delivering Ice Blue, Silver Grey, or Bronze tones.

L4
High-Index Precision Prescription Substrate

High-clarity optical resin tailored to the wearer's specific corrective prescription parameters.

L5
Polarized Anti-Glare Filtration Grid

Eliminates horizontal glare from wet asphalt, oceanic reflection, and snowscapes.

L6
Inner Multi-Coat Anti-Reflective Backing

Suppresses back-surface reflections and ghost images entering the ocular field from behind.

ENGINEERED EXCELLENCE

Designed for Everyday Life, Perfected by Science

Six core capabilities that elevate ChromaVision from a visionary sketch to an intelligent eyewear breakthrough.

Prescription Support

Fully compatible with customized ophthalmic prescriptions — including single-vision, astigmatic toric, bifocal, and progressive multifocal requirements. No bulky inserts or double lenses needed.

  • High-index thin profile (1.67 / 1.74)
  • Free-form digital surfacing
  • Zero distortion field-of-view

Automatic Colour Adaptation

Continuous, microsecond optical transition that adjusts autonomously to varying weather conditions without tactile buttons, external remotes, or smartphone battery drains.

  • Instant molecular reaction
  • Gradual non-jarring fade curve
  • Friction-free zero-touch UX

Temperature Response

Calibrated thermo-responsive pigments react to ambient environmental heat: Ice Blue provides snowy contrast in the cold, Silver Grey offers neutral clarity in mild weather, and Bronze enriches warm horizons.

  • Cold mode: Light Ice Blue (<12°C)
  • Mild mode: Silver Grey (18°C–24°C)
  • Warm mode: Bronze (>28°C)

Sunlight Response & Override

Advanced photochromic silver halide nanocrystals rapidly darken the lenses when exposed to direct solar UV radiation, overriding temperature colors to create an impenetrable dark protective tint.

  • Automatic intense sun override
  • Dark Black deep polarization
  • Rapid attenuation within 15 seconds

UV Protection Goal

Engineered with medical-grade UV400 filtration as an uncompromising baseline — blocking 100% of UVA and UVB radiation across every single tint shade, from transparent indoor to full dark blackout.

  • 100% UVA/UVB bio-barrier (400nm)
  • Prevents long-term retinal fatigue
  • Meets ANSI and ISO ocular standards

Stylish Unisex Design

Sculpted from featherweight aerospace-grade beta-titanium and hypoallergenic bio-acetate. A balanced geometric frame geometry designed to flatter all facial contours with 19 grams of all-day comfort.

  • 19g ultra-lightweight chassis
  • Universal ergonomic nose bridge
  • Matte Obsidian & Silver accents

THE HORIZON

The ChromaVision R&D Trajectory

How we plan to evolve our academic concept into validated laboratory prototypes and accessible commercial eyewear.

PHASE 01

Experimental Lab Prototypes

Synthesizing initial benchtop test batches of micro-encapsulated thermochromic dyes suspended in optical-grade CR-39 and polycarbonate resins to benchmark color stability.

Target: Proof-of-concept coupon testing
PHASE 02

Advanced Nanomaterial Synthesis

Partnering with university materials science laboratories to explore graphene-infused matrices for accelerated thermal responsiveness and scratch-resistant hardness.

Target: Sub-5 second transition window
PHASE 03

Clinical Ocular Research

Conducting certified ophthalmological trials to validate contrast sensitivity, macular blue-light mitigation, and pupillary comfort across simulated extreme climates.

Target: Full ANSI Z80.3 & ISO 12312-1 validation
PHASE 04

Affordable Smart Eyewear

Our core startup philosophy: eliminating electronic microchips allows us to target an accessible price point, bringing smart adaptive eyewear to ordinary students and professionals.

Goal: Under $120 democratized target
PHASE 05

Expanding the Frame Silhouettes

Transitioning from our launch unisex geometric frame into sports wraparounds for cyclists and mountaineers, classic aviators, and ultra-minimal rimless titanium silhouettes.

Target: 4 versatile frame collections
